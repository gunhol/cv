# Homepage brief

## Job and audience

Read-mode homepage for recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leaders assessing Gunnar Hollander. Visitors should quickly understand his experience, recognize his current Sanitas role, and contact him. Email is the primary action; printing or saving the complete CV is secondary.

## Outcome and proof

The first viewport must communicate Gunnar's name, Forward Deployed Engineer title, December 2026 start at Sanitas, applied customer-facing focus, and decade of software experience across Swiss banking and healthcare. Use the supplied career history, four technology groups, two education entries, and three languages as proof.

The preceding Lead Software Engineer record includes setting up and delivering Offer & Application projects with business units, plus sharing and improving AI tooling, solutions, and engineering pipelines. Do not invent project outcomes, adoption figures, testimonials, clients, or new-role responsibilities.

## Scope and boundaries

Build one production-ready responsive homepage with a clean printed equivalent. Replace the current dark visual identity while preserving factual content, the public name, email address, location, domain, and GitHub Actions deployment. Keep the page minimal and fast, with static content and no unnecessary client JavaScript. Do not turn sections into a dashboard of nested cards or add decorative animation.

The owner confirmed this brief on 2026-11-07, including the additional Lead Software Engineer responsibilities, and subsequently directed that the Forward Deployed Engineer role be presented as current.

## States and ranges

Show nine experience entries: the current Forward Deployed Engineer role plus eight previous roles. Keep entries concise by default; use native, keyboard-accessible disclosure for long responsibility lists. Mark the current role with explicit text, not color alone. Print expands all details, removes navigation and action controls, and avoids dependence on background colors. Long company names and German qualification names must wrap without collision. There are no loading, empty, permission, or server-error states on this static surface.

## Interaction and layout

Use ordinary section anchors and semantic document headings. On desktop, follow the selected sketch's header, profile record, and stacked career records. On mobile, stack the profile and current-role panels, keep targets touch-friendly, and provide a thumb-reachable persistent email action without obscuring content. Disclosure needs clear expanded, collapsed, hover, and focus states. Motion is limited to subtle press feedback and is removed under reduced-motion preferences. Printing exposes the complete linear document.

## Constraints and open decisions

Astro and TypeScript generate static HTML/CSS with no client UI framework, using the Node.js 26 preference and GitHub Actions deployment. Use a comfortable reading measure, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic landmarks, and accessible native controls. The structured data records Lead Software Engineer through November 2026 and Forward Deployed Engineer as current from December 2026; preserve that chronology. Final profile wording may be refined without adding claims beyond this brief.
